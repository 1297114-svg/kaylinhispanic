# Sources

- World Bank Indicators API: https://api.worldbank.org/
- World Bank country and indicator data endpoint format: `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/{country_code}/indicator/{indicator_code}?format=json`

## Country selection
The app uses World Bank ISO-3 country codes for the 20 Spanish-speaking countries. Brazil (`BRA`) is separately labeled as a Portuguese-speaking Latin American comparison.

## Indicator codes
- Life expectancy: `SP.DYN.LE00.IN`
- Unemployment, national estimate: `SL.UEM.TOTL.NE.ZS`
- GDP per capita: `NY.GDP.PCAP.CD`
- Crude birth rate: `SP.DYN.CBRT.IN`
- Net migration: `SM.POP.NETM`
- Intentional homicide rate: `VC.IHR.PSRC.P5`
- Consumer price inflation: `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG`
- Central government debt: `GC.DOD.TOTL.GD.ZS`
- Total reserves including gold: `FI.RES.TOTL.CD`
- External balance on goods and services: `NE.RSB.GNFS.ZS`
